##### A New Step in My Journey: Accepted into the Data Engineering Pilipinas × DataCamp Scholarship Program

On October 9, I discovered an opportunity that felt closely connected to my goals: the Data Engineering Pilipinas × DataCamp Scholarship Program. The next day, I submitted my application. Less than three days later, I received the wonderful news that I had been accepted.

This scholarship is another step toward my goal of becoming a software engineer. It gives me the chance to strengthen my foundation in data and technology while continuing to grow as a learner.

I’m grateful to [Data Engineering Pilipinas](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dataengineeringpilipinas/) and [DataCamp](https://www.linkedin.com/school/datacampinc/) for helping make learning opportunities more accessible to aspiring tech professionals like me.

I’m looking forward to learning, growing, and making the most of this opportunity. Here’s to continuous learning and new beginnings. All glory belongs to God.
