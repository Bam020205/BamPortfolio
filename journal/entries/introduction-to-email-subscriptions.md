I’ve added an email subscription option to Yohanan Times so readers can hear about new Journal entries without checking the website every day. Here is how it works and what to expect.

## Joining the list

At the bottom of the Journal and each article, readers can enter their email address and press **Subscribe**. A confirmation message is then sent to their inbox. They need to open that message and confirm their address before joining the Yohanan Times list. This extra step helps ensure that each subscriber actually asked to receive the emails.

## When I publish

I write and publish an entry through the Journal CMS. The website then adds it to the Journal page and its RSS feed. The feed gives each new article a title, date, short description, and link. The subscription form and list are managed in MailerLite.

For now, I prepare the announcement email in MailerLite after publishing. It includes a preview of the article and a button that leads to the full entry. I review it and send it to confirmed subscribers. Publishing an article alone does not send an email.

## Staying in control

Subscribers can use the **Unsubscribe** link in an announcement to leave the list. The Journal remains available to everyone on the website, whether or not they subscribe.

I hope this makes it easier to follow new reflections, devotions, and stories as I add them. Thank you for reading along.
