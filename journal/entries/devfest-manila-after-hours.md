At DevFest Manila 2026 After Hours, I got to see how a team can take an idea all the way to a live application with Google Antigravity.

I especially enjoyed the hands-on workflows led by Jeremy Ephraim Villa and Sermil Matoto. They showed how to coordinate AI agents, build a full-stack application, review its architecture, deploy cloud infrastructure, and verify the live result.

What stayed with me was the role of human review. Even with agents helping at each stage, a developer still has to decide whether the application works as intended.

Thank you to the DevFest Manila organizers and speakers for an evening of learning and building.
