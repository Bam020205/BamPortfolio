Behind the lens at Hacktoberfest 2026

Abram Seneca · November 24, 2026 · 1 min readShare

Share this article

Save Story image

I volunteered as a photographer with AWS Student Builder Group–Tempest, PLMun, at Hacktoberfest 2026 at National University–Las Piñas.

The photos tell different parts of the day. There are lanyards laid out on a table, a speaker at the microphone, rows of people listening, and participants gathered around their laptops.

I was glad to contribute my skills through the lens. Photography gave me a way to take part in the tech community and leave people with a record of the day we shared.
