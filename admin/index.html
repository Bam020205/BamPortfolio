Writing desk

Write an entry, upload your cover photo, and preview the article before publishing.

Open editor
First sign-in
  1. Sign in to GitHub as Bam020205 and authorize a writing token.
  2. Select Only select repositories and choose YohananTimes-Writing. Allow Contents and Pull requests read and write access. Generate and copy the token.
  3. Open the editor, choose Sign In Using Access Token, and paste it there.

Write and publish

Open Journal and create an entry. Fill in the title, publication date, cover photo, image description, and body. Use the preview to review your work. Choose Save to keep a private draft. When you’re ready, choose Publish Entry, or use Save and Publish from the Save menu. To make a correction, open the entry in Journal, edit it, and publish your update. The article keeps its original address.
