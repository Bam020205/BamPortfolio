Robotics research

DeTerminator

An Arduino-based rover that explores carbon dioxide and humidity as possible indicators of termite activity.

Arduino · C++ · Environmental sensors

The build

I programmed, integrated the hardware, and built the rover, named Terry. It collects environmental readings from sites where termite activity is suspected.

The science fair

DeTerminator earned fourth place in the individual Robotics and Intelligent Machines category at the 2025 Division Science and Technology Fair in Bulihan, Silang, Cavite.

Video description

The original presentation introduces Terry, shows the rover and its assembly, and explains the research and results. The recording includes on-screen captions.
